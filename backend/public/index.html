Why Easy Pay

We're a modern digital bank built around ACH — the backbone of US electronic payments.

ACH Capabilities

  • Standard ACH (1–3 business days)
  • Same-day ACH (where available)
  • Credit and debit ACH entries
  • NACHA-compliant processing
  • Clear status tracking (Pending → Settled)

Security & Compliance

This is a demonstration website only. No real bank accounts, no real money movement, and no real customer data are processed. In a production environment, Easy Pay would partner with licensed banks and comply with all applicable federal banking regulations, including BSA/AML and consumer protection rules.

How transfers work (demo)

  1. Sign up and get a simulated starting balance.
  2. Use Send / Receive / Transfer from the dashboard.
  3. Every action updates your local balance and appears in Account History.
  4. Data is stored only in your browser (localStorage).
